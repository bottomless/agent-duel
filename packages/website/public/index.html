How a duel works

  1. Open a project

    Point Agent Duel at any Git repo on your Mac and describe the change you want.

  2. Two agents, no names

    Two models work on your request side by side, each in its own worktree. You don't know which is which.

  3. Pick the winner

    Choose A, B, or call it a tie. The names are revealed, and you carry on from the version you picked.
